The Caah's Spirit

Where Shah Alam Finds Late-Night Comfort

Caah’s started from a simple belief: a community deserves a place that welcomes them at any hour. We realized that some of the best conversations, deepest design sketches, and local laughs happen over premium comfort food late at night.

We set up in Seksyen 7, Shah Alam, crafting a space with deep, luxury dark aesthetics, warm cinematic lights, and relaxing, low-fi lo-fi beats. We slow-ferment our unique brioche dough for up to 24 hours to give our donuts an unmatched cloud-like fluffiness that contrasts beautifully with our bold premium espresso roasts.

"It’s more than just dough and beans; it is a shared lifestyle of nightly sanctuary, focus, and warmth."
